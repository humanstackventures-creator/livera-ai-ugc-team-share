## Creative structure

- Original illustrated elderly East Asian character in a domestic outdoor setting.
- Story-first format with the Livera product reserved for the closing reveal.
- Vertical social format with captions, narration, music, and sound design.
- Real Livera pouch media is used for the closing product shot.

## Higgsfield workflow

1. Seedance-based visual chapters were generated for the story.
2. The narration was rebuilt first using a mature female voice appropriate to the character.
3. Lip-sync chapters were regenerated against the locked narration.
4. Captions were rebuilt from the final audio transcript rather than from a draft script.
5. Chapters were assembled with music, sound effects, and the real product close.

This sequence is the core correction: lock voice first, then lip-sync, then captions.
