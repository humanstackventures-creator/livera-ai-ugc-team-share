# Review Checklist

## Creative

- [ ] Hook is clear and attention-holding within the first three seconds.
- [ ] Pain/problem is understandable before Livera is introduced.
- [ ] Scene changes feel active without becoming hard to follow.
- [ ] Product reveal feels earned rather than inserted.

## Voice, lip-sync, and captions

- [ ] Voice age and tone suit the character.
- [ ] Spoken words match visible mouth movement closely.
- [ ] Captions match the final spoken audio.
- [ ] Captions remain readable in the TikTok/Reels safe area.

## Product

- [ ] Real Livera pouch is identifiable.
- [ ] Pouch proportions, materials, label, and branding remain accurate.
- [ ] No pills or unrelated supplement forms appear.
- [ ] Claims stay within the approved Livera label and compliance guidance.

## Publishing

- [ ] Final owner approval recorded.
- [ ] Platform-required AI disclosure applied.
- [ ] Music and sound effects cleared for the destination platform.
- [ ] Final export checked on a mobile device.
